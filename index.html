    How It Works

    This wedge pillow works by elevating your upper body, which reduces pressure on your airways and promotes better airflow for easier breathing.

    The gentle incline and curved neck support align your spine and jaw in an ideal position, helping to prevent airway obstruction and reducing strain on neck and jaw muscles.

    This optimal posture eases tension, encourages natural alignment, and supports restful, uninterrupted sleep.
